# Brag Plan: Kinoir

## What is this app?
Kinoir is a local-first macOS media library and player that brings local files and cloud links into one cinematic catalogue, with offline semantic search and an optional library-grounded AI Concierge.

## Planning rubric
1. **What is the app?** A local-first library and player for movies and shows on a Mac or in connected cloud storage.
2. **Funniest or most impressive claim:** “No service in the middle.”
3. **Visual hook:** The graphite smoked-glass interface, dense poster shelves, and embossed charcoal K icon.
4. **Actual UI to show:** The featured home screen, the poster-library hover preview, and the local AI Concierge recommendation panel.
5. **Shortest satisfying video:** 20 seconds; enough room for the privacy hook, three real product moments, and a clean brand lockup.
6. **Tone:** polished preset with a quiet cinematic macOS product-film direction.
7. **Audio feel:** restrained 110 BPM electronic bed, three low-risk tactile cues, and subtle music-reactive background light.
8. **Share caption:** Built Kinoir: a local-first home for the films and shows you already have. Search offline, ask your own library, and keep the service out of the middle.
9. **User flow:** Open the library → search by remembered mood/detail → ask the local Concierge → choose a title to play.

## The angle
The collection already belongs to the viewer; Kinoir simply gives it the interface it deserves. The film moves from personal ownership (“Your films. Your files.”), through the real library and search experience, into the local Concierge, then lands on the privacy promise: no Kinoir account and no service in the middle.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
On near-black graphite, the two source-site lines arrive with deliberate weight: “Your films.” then “Your files.” The Kinoir K sits as a smoked-glass object beside them, catching a cool silver rim.

## Key moments (the middle)

- The real home UI pushes into focus, showing the featured title, Continue Watching, and the app’s glass navigation.
- A remembered mood is typed into a faithful Kinoir search field: “quiet science fiction”; relevant mood tags settle beneath it while the real poster library remains visible.
- The real Concierge panel slides over the collection and resolves a library-only recommendation, emphasising “Runs locally via Ollama · library-only.”

## Outro / punchline
The UI falls away into the embossed K. “No service in the middle.” holds above a compact Kinoir / macOS / open-source lockup.

## User flow worth showing
Open the personal catalogue → search by mood rather than an exact title → ask the local, library-only Concierge → select a playable recommendation.
